What is this unit about?

University for the Creative Arts

  • This unit consists of a period of sustained, individually negotiated research on a subject related to thecontextual and/ or theoretical concerns of your discipline pathway, and towards the provision of astructured critical reflection on the intellectual underpinnings of your final major project.
  • In terms of the relationship between your writing and your practice, this may include reflection yourfinal major project itself, a specific role aligned with your practice (E.G. animator, level designer, 3Dartist or character artist), or your general area of practice (games arts, games animation, gamesdevelopment, games design).
  • In devising and effecting a written response to such research and its influence on or relevance to yourpractice, you will be expected to reflect upon and articulate a clear and sustained argument. Yourargument must conform to academic conventions and demonstrate an understanding of the subjectarea through selected, managed, and detailed analysis and evaluation.

Unit Project and Assessment: Structure

University for the Creative Arts

  • The structure of an academic text must contain multiple elements that come together to forma coherent whole.
  • With a reflective essay of this scale (3000-4000 words), the ability to structure your argumentis important. Although we do not wish to be prescriptive, as a guide, you may find the followingstructure useful:
  • Question and/or title of reflective essay
  • Introduction
  • Literature Review (including theoretical and conceptual framework)
  • Case Studies (analysis of examples of existing practice)
  • Conclusion and Reflectionon Practice

Structure 1: Question or Title

University for the Creative Arts

  • The question or title you have set yourself and agreed with your supervisor should appear clearly onthe front page of the essay and should be directly referred to throughout.
  • Your question or title should include key concepts and ideas from scholarly discourses and giveinsight into the areas of practice you are analysing.
  • In addition, your title should provide an idea of how the project is relevant to your practice.
  • The introduction is usually written (or at least revised) once an academic submission is complete.
  • Introductions should include a breakdown of the key issues and terms referenced in thequestion/title, or implied by it and note the role of these elements in your submission.
  • It should also include an outline of the project; an outline of the theoretical framework to beemployed; and the case studies (usually examples of practice) to be considered, and why they havebeen chosen.

Structure 3: Literature Review and Theoretical Framework (1000 words)

University for the Creative Arts

A literature review often features as an early section in an academic essay. In a literature review you should include an outline and evaluation of existing theories and publications that are closely related to your research topic, which is, in turn, connected to your practice.

You should also make your own theoretical assumptions and allegiances as explicit as possible.. You must demonstrate that you are capable of thinking critically, with insight and in an evaluative manner about the issues raised in previous research.

Please be aware that literature reviews are not simply summaries of existing academic research, nor are they lists of unexamined quotes. You must identify the questions, challenges, and linkages that key research texts pose and create for your practice, and apply them your analysis of examples, including reflection on your practice, or area of practice.

Structure 4: Case studies (analyses of existing examples – 1000 words)

  • Applying and testing the theories you have discussed in the theoretical framework/literature review to casestudies (usually existing examples of practice), is a key part of your submission.
  • Key questions to consider: How do key theories connect with existing practice, and with your own? Whatquestions, linkages, or challenges does your literature review raise for existing practices, and how might thisinform your own work?
